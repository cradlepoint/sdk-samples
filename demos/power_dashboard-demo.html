Which readings each model reports
  • Watts, voltage, and current: R980, S400.
  • Watts and voltage: R920, R1900.
  • Watts only: S700, IBR1700, IBR600C, E3000.
  • Not supported: IBR900.

For R920 and R1900 the app turns on the ADC channel it reads voltage from.

How it decides

Two thresholds split voltage into three levels. The lights and the alerts use the same levels.

Three levels

At or above 12.1 V is high, at or above 11.5 V is medium, below that is low. No reading, or 0 V, shows a black circle and sends no alert.

Alert on change

An alert goes out each time the level changes. At startup it alerts only if the level is not high.

Data kept

The last 24 hours stay in memory for the page, and 30 days are saved to disk. Live view is not saved.

Model aware

It detects the router model and reads the right paths for it. IBR900 is not supported, and the app exits there.

Setup notes

This app has no SDK Data fields to set.

  • Set fields in Ericsson NetCloud Manager under the device or group's SDK Data. Interval, lights, and signal are checked at startup, so restart the app after changing them. Thresholds are read on every pass.
  • Open the dashboard at http://router_ip:<port>. The port is in the app log. LAN clients need zone forwarding from the Primary LAN Zone to the Router Zone. Remote access works through NetCloud Manager Remote Connect LAN Manager with host 127.0.0.1 and the app port.
  • The app waits for 30 seconds of router uptime before it starts.
  • Needs router firmware 7.26 or later.
