Cell Survey

At a site with weak signal, you're guessing which carrier is best. Cell Survey has the router's own modem scan the air and rank every carrier in range, strongest signal first — with one modem and no SIM needed. Then you can lock onto the winner or activate its eSIM profile from the same page the router serves.

No SIM needed

The modem scans the air itself, so it hears every carrier's towers with no SIM installed and no cellular connection.

Ranked by signal

Carriers sort strongest first, each tower with its band, channel, and a plain quality label.

Switch to the winner

Lock the modem to that carrier's PLMN, or activate its eSIM profile, in one click.

How it decides

Asks the modem directly

It has the modem itself scan the air and report every cell it can hear, so it works with no SIM in the modem, disconnected, or on standby. Supported on the Ericsson 5GD / 5GF modules.

Groups by PLMN

Each cell's MCC+MNC is matched against a bundled worldwide table to name the carrier and country — no internet needed. Cells roll up per carrier.

Ranks by best RSRP

Carriers sort by their strongest cell. The reading gets a label: Excellent ≥ -80, Good ≥ -90, Fair ≥ -100, else Poor (dBm).

Activates a matching eSIM

It matches a surveyed carrier to an installed operational eUICC profile by name. Activating enables that profile; the modem re-registers on the carrier (and may briefly drop or reset). A carrier whose profile is already active shows an eSIM active badge.

SDK Data settings

Nothing is required. The app runs on its in-code defaults, and the Schedule page writes its own field for you.

SettingRequiredDefaultWhat it does
cell_survey_web_port No First free port from 8000, then saved Web UI port (1 to 65535). Set it to pin the port; delete it and restart to pick one automatically again.
cell_survey_port No None Deprecated older name for the web port, used only when cell_survey_web_port is not set.
cell_survey_schedule No None (startup and scheduled surveys off) JSON saved by the Schedule page: whether to survey on startup and on a schedule, the timing, and what to do after the survey.
  • startup_enabled, schedule_enabled — true or false (default false)
  • schedule_cron — a cron expression such as */30 * * * *
  • startup_action, schedule_action — none (default), lock the best carrier's PLMN, or esim to activate its eSIM profile
  • Set fields in Ericsson NetCloud Manager under the device or group's SDK Data, then restart the app.
  • Open the web UI at http://router_ip:<port>. The port is in the app log at startup.
  • If LAN clients can't reach the page, add a zone firewall forward from the Primary LAN Zone to the Router Zone.
  • The survey needs an Ericsson 5GD or 5GF modem. eSIM buttons appear only on an eSIM modem with a profile installed for that carrier.
